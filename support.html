Fix common issues

Troubleshooting

1

Check Motion & Fitness permission

This is the most common cause of step counting issues. Without it, CoreMotion cannot read the pedometer.

Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → JustSteps ✓
2

Check notification permission

Required for goal-reached alerts and daily reminders.

Settings → Notifications → JustSteps → Allow Notifications ✓
3

Check HealthKit access (for history)

If step history is missing or not syncing with Apple Watch data, verify Health access is granted.

Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → JustSteps
4

Disable Low Power Mode

iOS significantly restricts background activity in Low Power Mode. This can delay or prevent background step updates and notifications.

Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode → Off
5

Force quit and reopen

Swipe up from the bottom (or double-tap the Home button), swipe up on JustSteps to force quit it, then reopen. This refreshes the live update connection.

6

Restart your iPhone

A full restart often resolves persistent CoreMotion or HealthKit sync issues. Hold the side button + volume down, slide to power off, then power back on.

How it works

About the app

📱

iPhone (today)

Today's step count is read directly from CoreMotion — your iPhone's motion coprocessor. This runs independently of HealthKit and doesn't require the Health app.

❤️

HealthKit (history)

Past days are fetched from HealthKit, which aggregates data from all your devices including Apple Watch. HealthKit is always the source of truth for historical data.

⌚

Apple Watch

Step count syncs from iPhone to Watch in real time using WatchConnectivity. Watch complications update automatically throughout the day.

🔔

Notifications

Background delivery via HKObserverQuery wakes the app when HealthKit receives new step data. The goal notification fires the moment you cross the threshold — even with the app closed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

JustSteps ("the app") is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. All information stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account via Apple's HealthKit and SwiftData frameworks.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or registration is required
  • No personal information is collected or stored on external servers
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs are used
  • No crash reporting SDKs that transmit data externally
  • No advertising networks or tracking pixels

Data stored on your device

JustSteps stores step counts and walking distance locally using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly back up your iPhone to iCloud, in which case it is covered by Apple's iCloud privacy practices.

Step data is also read from and written to Apple HealthKit. Your HealthKit data is governed by Apple's Health privacy policy. JustSteps only reads step count and walking distance; it does not write any data to HealthKit except to update today's step count for widget display purposes.

HealthKit

JustSteps requests read access to Step Count and Walking + Running Distance from HealthKit. This data is used solely within the app to display your step count, distance, and history. It is not shared with any third party.

Motion & Fitness

JustSteps uses CoreMotion to count steps directly from your iPhone's motion sensor. This requires Motion & Fitness permission. The data is processed locally and not transmitted anywhere.

Notifications

JustSteps sends local push notifications for goal completion and daily reminders. These are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any external servers.

Children's privacy

JustSteps is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change. We will never introduce data collection without explicit user consent.
